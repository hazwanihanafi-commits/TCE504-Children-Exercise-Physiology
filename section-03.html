Section screen 8: Growth in

Height

Height increases rapidly during the first two years of life

Linear growth velocity decreases rapidly from 30 cm a year during the first months of life, to approximately 9 cm a year at the age of 2, and to 7 cm a year at 5 years of age

The linear growth rate then continues at approximately 5.5 cm a year before slowing slightly just before puberty

The peak height velocity is about 14-16 years in girls and 16-18 years in boys.

Some boys do not reach their full height until their early 20s.

Section screen 9: For girls,

the pubertal growth spurt begins at approximately 10 years of age, reaches a peak of approximately 10.5 cm a year at the age of 12, and then decreases toward zero around the age of 15.

For boys,

the growth velocity increases sharply around the age of 12, reaches a peak velocity of 12 cm a year at the age of 14, and then decelerates toward zero around the age of 17
