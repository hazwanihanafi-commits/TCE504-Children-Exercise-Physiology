CLEAR SCIENCE FACTS · SECTION 1

Growth is not maturation

What the science says

Growth refers to measurable changes in body size; maturation describes progression toward the biologically mature state. Children of the same age may mature at different rates.

Apply it

Chronological age alone does not establish readiness for a particular exercise load.

INTERACTIVE CHALLENGE · SECTION 1

Growth vs maturation

A 12-year-old and a 14-year-old have the same height. Does that mean they are at the same biological maturity?

Mechanism-based explanation

No. Height or chronological age alone cannot determine biological maturation.
