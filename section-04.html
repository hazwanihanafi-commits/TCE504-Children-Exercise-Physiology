CLEAR SCIENCE FACTS · SECTION 4

Heart rate and stroke volume

What the science says

At a given submaximal absolute workload, children often exhibit higher heart rates and lower absolute stroke volumes than adults.

Apply it

Interpret heart rate with age, body size, fitness and exercise conditions in mind.

INTERACTIVE CHALLENGE · SECTION 4

Cardiovascular mechanisms

Why is heart rate generally higher in children than adults at a comparable absolute workload?

Mechanism-based explanation

Children generally have lower stroke volume and may compensate with a higher heart rate.
