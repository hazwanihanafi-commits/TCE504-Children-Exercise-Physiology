CLEAR SCIENCE FACTS · SECTION 2

Maturation varies

What the science says

Pubertal timing and tempo vary widely. Height and body mass alone do not establish skeletal or sexual maturity.

Apply it

Use developmentally appropriate coaching and avoid judging ability from age alone.

Section screen 6: Fat and Growth

At birth, 10% to 12% of total body weight is fat

At physical maturity, the fat content = 15% of total body weight for males, 25% for females

Cause: Hormonal differences

When girls reach puberty, their estrogen concentrations and tissue exposure increase, promoting the deposition of body fat
